# FP-02 - The Fool's Cipher



## Scenario



The invitation recovered during the previous investigation contains a clue that may unlock another part of The Fool's trail.



A strange encoded message has been recovered. Its contents are not immediately readable, and the correct key is needed to uncover what it contains.



## Objective



Analyze the encoded message, identify the transformations applied to it, and recover its original contents.



Submit the FP-02 flag to CTFd to complete this challenge.



## Artifact



`fools-cipher.txt`



Location: `artifact/fools-cipher.txt`



## Difficulty



Easy



## Category



Cryptography



## Prerequisite



FP-01 - The Invitation



Carry forward the key or clue recovered from FP-01.



## Intended Skills



- Recognizing Base64 encoding

- Identifying a Vigenere cipher

- Using a previously recovered key

- Reversing a sequence of encoding and encryption steps

- Reading recovered investigation evidence



## Handoff



The recovered message provides the information required to continue to FP-03 - The Locked Door.



Keep the recovered connection information available for the next stage.
