The invitation concealed more than one secret.

Ashes remember what the Fool forgot.
Pages hide more than their written words.
Red clues wait where ordinary eyes pass.
In silence, the invitation speaks.
Look closely before moving on.
Fools have always remembered one day.
In that day, another lock awaits.
Read the beginnings, not only the ends.
Silent marks can guide the way.
The Fool never leaves one clue alone.
