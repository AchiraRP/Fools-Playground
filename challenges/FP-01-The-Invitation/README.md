# FP-01 — The Invitation

## Scenario

An invitation has been recovered from the scene of the incident.

It appears ordinary at first glance, but The Fool was known for leaving
information where investigators were least likely to look.

## Objective

Investigate the invitation carefully and recover the information hidden
within it.

Submit the flag you discover to complete FP-01.

## Artifact

`invitation.png`

## Difficulty

Easy

## Category

Steganography

## Handoff

The information recovered from this challenge will help you proceed to
the next stage of Fool's Playground.
